# Bin It Right UI Mock-up Notes

## Shared Palette and Typography

The interface uses a civic street-sign aesthetic designed for quick reading by Long Beach residents rather than a promotional startup style.
The page background is a cool pale concrete grey-blue (`#EEF2F1`) paired with deep harbour navy (`#0E2A3B`) for primary text and borders.
Cart destinations are represented by flat, high-contrast colour slabs with no gradients: recycling blue (`#1D5FD1`), organics green (`#2F7D3A`), trash slate (`#4A5560`), hazardous amber (`#F2A900`), and drop-off teal (`#0F766E`).
Headings and cart slabs are set in Overpass, a highway-signage sans-serif typeface that preserves clarity at large sizes.
Body copy, verbatim municipal quotes, source citations, and interactive controls are set in Atkinson Hyperlegible for maximum character distinction and accessibility.
The layout avoids cream backgrounds, terracotta accents, dark neon themes, all-caps eyebrow labels, gradient washes, and repetitive rounded card grids.

## Phone Mock-up (`design/mockups/phone-answer.jpg`)

The phone screen follows a single-column portrait layout sized around 390 by 844 pixels with generous touch targets and vertical spacing.
A compact top header displays the "Bin It Right" wordmark on the left, a "Long Beach - City-serviced carts" jurisdiction chip in the centre, and an "EN | ES" language toggle on the right.
Beneath the header sits a prominent search input labelled "What are you throwing away?" followed by a horizontal row of large tappable quick-search chips for "Pizza box", "Battery", "Plastic bag", and "Coffee grounds".
The primary answer appears immediately below the search controls as an edge-to-edge solid organics green (`#2F7D3A`) slab reading "Green cart" in extra-large white Overpass type, with the subtitle "Organics" and a simple leaf icon underneath.
Directly under the green slab, the matched item title "Food-soiled paper (napkins, paper towels, pizza boxes)" introduces a verbatim quote block displaying `"Food-soiled paper (napkins, paper towels, pizza boxes)"`.
Below the quote, a provenance line reads "City of Long Beach - Residential Organics (Green Cart) Collection - fetched Nov 18, 2026" alongside an underlined "Open the City's page" link.
A quiet footer note at the bottom states that rules apply to City-serviced accounts and that private haulers may differ.

## Desktop Mock-up (`design/mockups/desktop-overview.jpg`)

The desktop screen uses a landscape 1440 by 900 two-column layout anchored by a full-width top header bar with the "Bin It Right" wordmark, the "Long Beach - City-serviced carts" chip, and the "EN | ES" language switch.
The left column mirrors the phone lookup workflow, containing the large search input, the row of tappable item chips, and the active answer panel.
In the desktop view, the active answer shows a solid recycling blue (`#1D5FD1`) slab reading "Blue cart - Recycle" for the matched item "Glass bottles and jars", followed by the verbatim City rule quote, fetch date, source link, and hauler disclaimer note.
The right column is titled "Browse all official rules" and lists the City of Long Beach rules grouped under solid colour bars for each cart category (`#1D5FD1`, `#2F7D3A`, `#4A5560`, `#F2A900`, and `#0F766E`).
Each rule row in the right-hand directory pairs the official item wording with a compact source link on the right edge for direct verification.

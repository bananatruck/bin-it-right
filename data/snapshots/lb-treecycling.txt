2027 Holiday Treecycling
Annual Holiday Treecycling Drop-Off Program
Release Date: 2026-11-19
The Department of Energy & Environmental Services will offer Holiday Treecycling, giving Long Beach residents a convenient, responsible way to drop off live holiday trees for recycling at no cost.
Residents are asked to:
Remove decorations
Take the tree out of the stand
Cut the tree in half if it is over 12 feet tall
Leave trees unwrapped
The City recycles nearly 160 tons of holiday trees into compost or landscaping mulch each year, which equates to about 14,000 trees. Proper disposal of holiday trees through Treecycling drop-off locations prevents fire hazards and reduces illegally dumped trees in alleys and the public right-of-way.
Residents are strongly encouraged to use designated citywide drop-off locations. This is the preferred method, ensuring timely, efficient disposal and diverting material from landfills. If you can't visit a drop-off location, a one-time curbside collection will be available on Saturday, Jan. 9, 2027. Residents with City refuse, recycling, and organics service must place their trees at their regular collection spot by 6 a.m. on Jan. 9 to support collection. If collection is needed after January 9, 2027, residents should submit a Special Collections request.
Flocked trees are accepted.
Natural wreaths without nonorganic decorations are allowed in organics (green) carts.
Drop-off sites are available at 13 locations throughout the city; see below for the exact locations.
Drop-Off Collection Dates & Hours:
Dec. 26, 2026 – Jan. 8, 2027
Monday-Friday: 9:00 am - 5:00 pm
Saturday-Sunday: 9:00 am - 2:00 pm

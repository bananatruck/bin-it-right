Household Hazardous Waste
What is Household Hazardous Waste (HHW)?
Leftover household products that can catch fire, react, or explode under certain conditions, or that are corrosive or toxic. These items may not be placed in your trash or recycling carts.
Why is it important to dispose of HHW properly?
Dispose of HHW properly so toxic chemicals don’t end up where they can harm humans, wildlife, or the environment.
Examples of Household Hazardous Waste:
Paint and paint thinners
Fluorescent light bulbs
Household cleaners
Used motor oil and filters
Pool chemicals
Expired medications
Batteries
Weed killer/pesticides
Metal polishers
Anti-freeze
